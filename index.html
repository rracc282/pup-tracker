How it works

The goal

Teach Churro, in small calm steps, that being alone is safe, until she can manage up to 4 hours. Progress only counts when she stays relaxed. Going slowly is the method, so the app never pushes past what she has shown she can do.

One rep, start to finish

  • Tell the app how much time you have if it asks, then start the rep it suggests.
  • Do the rep: a cue practice, or leave for the time shown. Come back calmly with no big hello.
  • Tap how she did: Calm, Wobble (mild stress) or Upset. Add a note if useful. Notes and tags help whoever is helping you read the day.
  • The app works out the next rep and the wait before it.

The ladder: 38 steps

  • Steps 1 to 6: departure cues with her watching (coat, shoes, bag, keys, bolt, then all together). The aim is for the cues to become boring.
  • Steps 7 to 12: the same cues with her behind the closed door, so she only hears them.
  • Step 13: opening and closing the front door without leaving.
  • Steps 14 to 38: time outside, from 2 seconds up to 4 hours. Tap the progress card at the top to see every step.

Moving up, repeating, dropping back

  • Calm counts toward moving up: 2 calm reps in a row for coat, shoes and bag, 3 for everything else.
  • Wobble repeats the step and makes the next rep easier. A second wobble in a row drops her one step.
  • Upset drops her two steps and pauses training for 3 hours. A rough day is information, not failure.
  • Only reps at her working step can move her up, and no rep is ever harder than that step.

Why reps vary

About 60% of reps are at her working step, about 25% are shorter or easier on purpose to build confidence, and about 15% are no-leave decoys (open and close the door, no departure), so the cues never reliably mean "she is leaving". Session size (3 to 6 reps) and the break after it (45 to 90 minutes) also vary, so the day has no fixed script.

Pacing and limits

  • The wait after a rep is 2 minutes for quick calm reps, doubled after a wobble, and longer after longer steps. "Skip the wait" exists, but a shorter recovery makes wobbles more likely.
  • Daily limits: 15 quick reps (cues and steps up to 1 minute), plus 10 longer reps on home days (including Wednesdays), 8 on weekends and 5 on sitter days. There are also limits on step-ups per day.
  • The break between sessions is rest, not training. She can be with you, and you should not leave her alone by accident.

What does and does not move the ladder

  • Moves it: training reps (cue practice and time outside) done alone, logged on a normal day.
  • Logged, but never moves it: rest blocks (she is behind a closed door while you are home), real alone time (a sitter cancelled, an errand), reps on a medicated day, and reps you void.
  • Partner home: you both leave together. If one of you stays, she is not alone and it does not count. Tick "Both of us" in the note.
  • Real absences: keep them within her current step. Anything longer, take her with you or use a sitter.

Days and places

  • Every morning the app asks what kind of day it is (home, weekend or sitter) and where you will be. That sets the day's limits.
  • Each place has its own ladder. A new place starts three steps below your Home step.
  • Set my step (in More) lets you jump to a step by hand. The skipped steps show in orange on the bar, a manual step back shows in dark green, and a red tick marks where she had to drop back. The rules above carry on from the new step.

Best conditions

After a walk, relaxed, awake or just waking. Come back at the first sign of stress building, before panic.

Notifications

Next rep ready, time is up on long steps, a morning day-and-place question, a training reminder if there are no reps yet that day, and a summary nudge at 22:00. Nothing between 22:30 and 07:00 except a time-up.

For whoever is helping

  • Please follow the app's next rep rather than choosing steps by feel, and let her wobbles slow things down. Pushing on is what sets her back.
  • Keep reps boring: no big greetings or goodbyes.
  • Reps, notes and day settings sync between devices. A running timer lives on the device that started it, so finish a rep on the same device.
  • At the end of the day, the summary (from the 22:00 notification) can be shared with Claude.
