How it works

The idea

Teach Churro, in small calm steps, that being alone is safe, up to 4 hours. Only calm reps count, and the app never pushes past what she has shown.

One rep

  • Start the rep the app suggests (tap the box to see how to do it). Come back calmly, no big hello. Up to 10 seconds there is no timer. Longer reps have a timer, or tap "Skip the timer" if you are watching the camera.
  • Tap how she did: Calm, Wobble (mild stress) or Upset. Then say if she was awake or asleep, and add a note if useful.
  • The app works out the next rep and the wait.

The ladder: 52 steps

  • 1 to 6: cues while she watches. 7 to 12: the same cues behind the closed door. 13: open and close the front door. 14 to 52: time outside, from 2 seconds to 4 hours. Tap the top card to see every step.

Moving up

  • Calm reps in a row at the working step: 2 for coat, shoes and bag, 3 for the other cues, 4 for the front door, 3 for departures up to 1 minute, 2 up to 4 minutes, 3 beyond.
  • Front door: the calm reps must come from 2 different days, with at least 2 awake or dozing on each. Above it: at least 2 of the calm reps (1 when only 2 are needed) with her awake or dozing, because a sleeping dog may not notice the cue.
  • Step-ups per day: 3 on the first cues, 2 behind the door, 1 on the front door, 2 for departures up to 10 seconds, then 1 up to 1 minute, 2 up to 4 minutes and 1 beyond. After any wobble or upset, no step-ups until the next day.
  • These rules apply from 13 October. Earlier reps keep the rules she trained under.

When it goes wrong

  • Wobble: the count of calm reps restarts, the next rep is easier and the rest is doubled. A second wobble in a row drops her one or two steps.
  • Upset: drops her two steps and pauses training for 3 hours. A rough day is information, not failure.

Why reps vary

  • About 60% of reps are at her working step and 40% are shorter real departures (roughly a tenth to three quarters of it), so the cues never reliably mean "she is leaving". At 2 seconds there is nothing shorter. A rep that does not count is always followed by a working-step rep.
  • Warm-ups: each session starts with one or two easier reps that do not count. You get two after a wobble or upset the last training day, after 3 or more days away, or when you are one calm rep from moving up. While her step is only a few seconds, a warm-up may just be opening and closing the door.
  • Rests between reps grow with the time outside and vary at random. Sessions are 3 to 6 reps (5 to 8 up to 7 seconds), then a break of 45 to 90 minutes.

Limits and days

  • Each day: 15 quick reps (cues and steps up to 1 minute) plus 10 longer reps on home days, 8 on weekends and 5 on sitter days. After 20:30 or a second session, a wind-down card appears (tap "Keep training" to carry on). "Rest day" and "End of day summary" stop the day's training.
  • Every morning, pick the day type and where you are. Each place has its own ladder. "Set my step" in More moves her by hand.
  • Never moves the ladder: rest blocks, real alone time, medicated days, voided reps, and reps with your partner staying home.

For whoever is helping

  • Follow the app's next rep rather than choosing by feel, and let her wobbles slow things down. Keep reps boring: no big hellos or goodbyes. Best after a walk, relaxed and awake.
  • Everything syncs between devices. A running timer lives on the device that started it.
