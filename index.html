How it works

The idea

Teach Churro, in small calm steps, that being alone is safe, up to 4 hours. Only calm reps count, and the app never pushes past what she has shown.

One rep

  • Start the rep the app suggests (tap the box to see how to do it). Come back calmly, no big hello. Under 5 minutes there is no timer: you watch the camera and the planned time is logged. From 5 minutes on there is a timer, or tap "Skip the timer" if you are watching the camera.
  • Tap how she did: Calm, Alert (quiet, but early stress signs such as standing up or staring at the door), Wobble (mild stress) or Upset. Then say if she was awake or asleep, and add a note if useful.
  • The app works out the next rep and the wait.

The ladder: 52 steps

  • 1 to 6: cues while she watches. 7 to 12: the same cues behind the closed door. 13: open and close the front door. 14 to 52: time outside, from 2 seconds to 4 hours. Tap the top card to see every step.

Moving up

  • Calm reps in a row at the working step: 2 for coat, shoes and bag, 3 for the other cues, 4 for the front door, 3 for every departure.
  • Front door: the calm reps must come from 2 different days, with at least 2 awake or dozing on each. Above it: at least 2 of the calm reps (1 when only 2 are needed) with her awake or dozing, because a sleeping dog may not notice the cue.
  • Step-ups per day: 3 on the cues (she watches and behind the door), 1 on the front door, 2 for departures up to 10 seconds, then 1 up to 1 minute, 2 up to 4 minutes and 1 beyond. On departure steps she can end a day at most 1.5 times higher than the step she started it on, and gets only one step-up on the first day back after 2 or more days without training. The cue steps are not affected by either. After an upset, no step-ups until the next day.
  • After a long break: after 7 or more days without training at Home, she starts one step easier. Zurich is not changed.
  • These rules apply from 13 October. Earlier reps keep the rules she trained under.

When it goes wrong

  • Alert: the rep does not count and your calm count stays. The next rep repeats the same step. A second alert in a row counts as a wobble.
  • Wobble: the count of calm reps restarts, the next rep is easier and the rest is doubled. A second wobble in a row drops her one or two steps.
  • Upset: drops her two steps, pauses training for 3 hours and means no step-up for the rest of that day. A rough day is information, not failure.

Why reps vary

  • About 7 in 10 reps are at her working step and 3 in 10 are shorter real departures (roughly a tenth to three quarters of it), so the cues never reliably mean "she is leaving". At 2 seconds there is nothing shorter. A rep that does not count is always followed by a working-step rep.
  • Warm-ups: the day starts with one or two easier reps that do not count. You get two after a wobble or upset the last training day, after 3 or more days away, on the first day at a step she has not done yet, or when you are one calm rep from moving up. Later in the day, one more warm-up only after a gap of 3 hours or more. Meetings and lunch breaks carry on as normal. While her step is only a few seconds, a warm-up may just be opening and closing the door.
  • Rests between reps grow with the time outside and vary at random. Sessions are 3 to 6 reps (5 to 8 up to 7 seconds), then a break of 45 to 90 minutes.

Limits and days

  • Each day: 15 short reps (cues and steps up to 1 minute) plus 10 longer reps on home days, 8 on weekends and 5 on sitter days. When you reach the limit, the app goes straight to the end of day summary (you can still tap "Keep training anyway"). After 20:30 a wind-down card appears. Under More you can also turn on a stop after two sessions. "Rest day" and "End of day summary" stop the day's training.
  • Every morning, pick the day type and where you are. Each place has its own ladder. "Set my step" in More moves her by hand.
  • Never moves the ladder: rest blocks, real alone time, medicated days, voided reps, and reps with your partner staying home.

For whoever is helping

  • Follow the app's next rep rather than choosing by feel, and let her wobbles slow things down. Keep reps boring: no big hellos or goodbyes. Best after a walk, relaxed and awake.
  • Everything syncs between devices. A running timer lives on the device that started it.
