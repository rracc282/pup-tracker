How it works
  1. Pick the time you have. You get one rep. Do it, tap how she did. The next rep updates by itself.
  2. The first rungs are cue practice in your real order: coat, shoes, bag, keys, bolt. One cue at a time, then all five together. First with her watching, then with her behind the closed door. Never stack cues before each one is boring.
  3. You move on after 2 calm reps in a row for coat, shoes and bag, and 3 for keys, bolt, the stack and anything behind the door. At most 3 step-ups a day on the cue steps. That is also when you move from her watching to her only hearing it.
  4. After the cues, the rungs are seconds spent outside, growing slowly toward 4 hours. Most reps are your working step. Some are shorter or easier on purpose, and some are no-leave decoys (open and close the door), so the cues do not always mean you are going. After a wobble the next rep is always easier. Only reps at your working step move you up, and none is ever harder than that.
  5. Calm moves you up. A wobble repeats the step. Two wobbles drop one step. An upset drops two and pauses you for 3 hours.
  6. The app spaces the reps: 2 minutes after a calm quick rep (double after a wobble), longer after longer steps. After 5 in a row it asks for an hour off. Daily limits: 15 quick reps (cues and steps up to 1 minute) and 5, 8 or 10 longer reps (10 on Wednesdays). "Skip the wait" lets you start early, but she then has less time to recover and wobbles become more likely.
  7. Between training reps, do not leave her alone for longer than your current step. Until the ladder catches up, groceries, drinks and concerts mean taking her along or using a sitter. Real absences she cannot handle undo the training.
  8. Best conditions: after a walk, relaxed, awake or just waking, you return calmly. Come back at the first sign it is building, before panic.
  9. A rest block is separate: she is behind the closed door while you do something else at home. It never changes your step.
  10. With your partner home, you both leave together. If one of you stays, she is not alone and it does not count. Tick "Both of us" when you add a note.
  11. Zurich or anywhere new has its own ladder and starts three steps below your Home step. Run a rep on one device from start to finish. The timer lives on the device that started it, while reps and waits sync everywhere.
  • Day type matters. Home days allow 10 longer reps, weekends 8, sitter days 5. The app asks every morning. On a medicated day, reps are logged but do not move your step.
  • Notifications: next rep ready, time is up on long steps, a morning day-type question, a training reminder, and a summary nudge at 22:00. Nothing between 22:30 and 07:00 except a time-up.
